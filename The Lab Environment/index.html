The FortiGate 60E replaced the pfSense VM as the perimeter firewall. Its internal interface connects to Switch002 (3550) Fa0/24 over a dedicated point-to-point transit subnet (192.168.100.0/30) rather than sitting directly on the 192.168.50.0/24 management subnet. Switch002 handles inter-VLAN routing and forwards default-route traffic to the FortiGate; Switch001 and Switch003 reach the internet by routing through Switch002's VLAN 50 SVI.
